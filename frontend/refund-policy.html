Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Freshly Baked to Order

Every cake is freshly baked specifically for your order, so we're unable to offer refunds simply for a change of mind once baking has started.

2. Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your cake arrives damaged, or if you received the wrong flavour or design, please message us on WhatsApp within 2 hours of delivery with a photo. We'll offer a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

3. Late or Missed Delivery

If your order doesn't arrive within a reasonable time of the promised delivery window and you no longer need it, contact us and we'll work out a fair refund or reschedule.

4. Cancellations

Orders cancelled before baking begins are eligible for a full refund. Once your cake is in production, it can no longer be cancelled or refunded.

5. How Refunds Are Processed

Approved refunds for Cash on Delivery orders are settled directly with you. Refunds for online payments are credited back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

6. Contact Us

For any refund request, please reach out via the Contact Us page or WhatsApp us directly with your order details.
